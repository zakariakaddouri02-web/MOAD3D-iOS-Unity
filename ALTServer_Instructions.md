# AltServer Installation & Signing Guide

This document explains how to install **AltServer** / **AltStore** on your computer and use it to sign the `MOAD3D.ipa` generated by Codemagic, then install the app on your iPhone.

## 1. Install AltServer on your computer
- **macOS**: `brew install altserver` or download the .dmg from https://altstore.io.
- **Windows**: download the installer from https://altstore.io, install it, and make sure you have the **Apple iTunes** and **iCloud** apps (not the Microsoft‑Store versions).

## 2. Connect your iPhone
1. Connect the iPhone via USB (or enable Wi‑Fi sync).
2. Open iTunes/iCloud and verify the device appears.
3. Launch AltServer → *Install AltStore* on the connected device.
4. On the iPhone, go to *Settings → General → Device Management* and trust the developer profile.

## 3. Get the signed IPA
1. In Codemagic, after a successful **ios‑build**, download the artifact `Build/*.ipa`.
2. Save it locally, e.g. `C:\Users\USER\Downloads\MOAD3D.ipa`.

## 4. Sign & install with AltStore
1. Open **AltStore** on the iPhone → *My Apps* → press the **+** button.
2. Choose **Browse Files** and locate the downloaded `MOAD3D.ipa`.
3. Enter your Apple ID and an **app‑specific password** (generated at appleid.apple.com).
4. AltStore will sign the IPA with a free development certificate and install the app.

> **Note**: Free certificates expire after 7 days. Keep your iPhone connected to the computer at least once a week; AltStore will automatically renew the signing.

## 5. Verify the app
- Launch **MOAD 3D SCAN** on the iPhone.
- Test the three UI buttons:
  - ⚡ Flash – toggles the torch.
  - 🎯 150 Photos – starts capture mode, HUD shows progress, stops at 150.
  - 💾 Export GLB – creates a `scan_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.glb` file in `Documents/MOAD3D/` and opens the iOS share sheet.

## 6. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| *Unable to sign app* | Ensure 2‑factor authentication is enabled on your Apple ID and use an **app‑specific password**. |
| *Device not recognized* | Install the latest Apple iTunes & iCloud (not Windows Store versions). |
| *App fails to launch* | Verify that the bundle identifier in `ProjectSettings/ProjectSettings.asset` matches the one used in the provisioning profile. |
| *Certificate expires* | Re‑open AltStore weekly with the device connected to refresh the signing. |
